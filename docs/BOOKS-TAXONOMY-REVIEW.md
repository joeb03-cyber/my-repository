## Verdict

The 13-topic taxonomy is useful as a discovery prototype but should not be approved unchanged. The principal issue is assignment breadth, not merely naming: 81 of 165 books have four topics (the present cap), 25 have one, and 14 have none. “Philosophy, Reality & Meaning” contains 102 books and overlaps with nearly every other topic, so it currently behaves like a generic intellectual-interest flag rather than useful navigation.

This keeps the top level at 13 topics by merging Psychology/Trauma and splitting the unusably broad Philosophy/Reality category. Later facets can express Trauma, Nervous System, Longevity, Pilgrimage, Productivity, Nonduality, and other narrower lenses without overcrowding Library navigation.

## Assignment policy proposed before applying v2

- Give each book zero to two primary topics; allow a third only with strong, independently reviewable evidence.
- Score title/subtitle and repeated document-level evidence more heavily than isolated passage keywords.
- Treat zero-topic books as a review queue, not a reason to force a weak category.
- Keep topics editorially suggested until reviewed; store confidence and evidence with each relationship.
- Derive future passage-level facets separately from book-level navigation topics.
